## What is it?

TailPlume is a link in bio and digital business card system, tailored towards people who visit conventions.

## Why am I developing it?

After using other link in bio services I wasn't happy with the way they handled customisation, and many of them locked basic features behind a paywall. So I'm building my own that is easy to use while offering a reasonable degree of customisation.

## Where can I find it?

The landing page is live at [tailplume.io](https://tailplume.io), and sign-ups will open once the application goes live.
